## THE SHAPE

**Three moves. Say them on slide 1 so the room knows where you're going.**

1. **Ask questions instead of giving orders.** Slides 1 through 6.
2. **Let the expert help you build the expert.** Slides 7 and 8.
3. **Put them in a room and let them plan it.** Slides 9 and 10.

Then slide 11, after the close, after the applause. "One more thing."

## THE FOUR THINGS TO NOT FORGET

1. **The audience is the subject, not you.** Open on their best manager, not your resume.
2. **Don't say "Socratic method."** Say a teacher figured out you learn more from a good question than a good answer, then move.
3. **Slide 5 is the whole talk.** Build it one line at a time and hold after the third line.
4. **Take questions live off slide 10.** No Q&A slide.

---

## THE ONE SENTENCE THEY STEAL

> *"Before you start, ask me questions that would help you do this better."*

Say it twice. Once on slide 5, once on slide 10.
